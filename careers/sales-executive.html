Sales Executive

Tempe, AZ Full-time In person

01

Job description

The Inside Sales Executive is responsible for the full-cycle B2B federal sales process—from prospecting new leads and cold calling to presenting Better Direct’s products and services.

This onsite role is based at our Tempe, Arizona office. We are looking for an innovative, resilient salesperson who enjoys creating new opportunities, overcoming challenges, and contributing to a collaborative team.

02

Key responsibilities

  • Develop and close new business through phone, online collaboration, and in-person meetings.
  • Identify opportunities and build quotes for submission through federal contracts.
  • Build positive client relationships through excellent customer service.
  • Generate new business by cross-selling additional products.
  • Maintain relationships with account holders while managing multiple accounts.
  • Address issues and concerns proactively while coordinating with fellow sales account executives.
  • Manage executive accounts with a focus on client satisfaction and sales growth.
  • Maintain deal velocity throughout the sales pipeline.
  • Use available intelligence, outreach tools, and strategic follow-up to set, hold, and close appointments.
  • Achieve and exceed annual quota goals.
  • Maintain accurate, current data in the digital database and uphold data confidentiality.

03

Requirements

  • Demonstrated federal sales skills and a consistent record of exceeding quota with existing customers and new business.
  • A natural ability to build positive relationships with customers and prospects.
  • Ability to cold call a large number of accounts and create a pipeline of qualified opportunities.
  • Proficiency with standard corporate productivity tools, including Microsoft Office and email.
  • Microsoft Dynamics proficiency is a plus.
  • Excellent research abilities.
  • Coachable, reliable, self-motivated, and able to work independently and collaboratively.
