Systems Engineer

Tempe, AZFull-timeIn person

01

Job description

We are seeking a detail-oriented and highly motivated Systems Engineer to join our team. The ideal candidate will have at least two years of experience in system design, integration, and troubleshooting. This role involves working closely with cross-functional teams to ensure the smooth operation of IT systems, network infrastructure, and software applications. The Systems Engineer will play a key role in maintaining system performance, security, and reliability.

02

Key responsibilities

  • Design, implement, and maintain IT systems to ensure high availability and performance.
  • Analyze, troubleshoot, and resolve hardware, software, and networking issues.
  • Assist with system integrations, upgrades, and migrations.
  • Monitor system performance and implement improvements that optimize efficiency.
  • Develop and maintain system documentation, including network diagrams, standard operating procedures, and technical specifications.
  • Collaborate with software developers, network engineers, and security teams to enhance system functionality.
  • Perform regular system maintenance, patches, and updates to support security compliance.
  • Provide technical support and training to end users as needed.
  • Stay current with emerging technologies and industry best practices.

03

Qualifications

Required qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field—or equivalent experience.
  • Two or more years of experience in systems engineering, IT support, or a related role.
  • Strong knowledge of Windows, Linux, and networking principles including TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, and VPNs.
  • Active Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) certification.
  • Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • Familiarity with VMware, Hyper-V, Docker, Kubernetes, monitoring tools, and scripting languages such as PowerShell, Python, or Bash.
  • Knowledge of cybersecurity best practices and compliance standards.
  • Strong troubleshooting, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.

Preferred qualifications

  • CompTIA Security+, Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate, or AWS Certified Solutions Architect certification.
  • Experience with IT service management tools such as ServiceNow or Jira.
  • Experience with automation and configuration management tools including Ansible, Puppet, or Terraform.
