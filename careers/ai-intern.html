AI Intern

Tempe, AZ Internship In person

01

Job description

We are looking for a motivated AI Intern who is passionate about large language models, machine learning, and applied AI systems. This internship focuses on building real-world AI systems—not just experimenting with models.

You will work on open-source LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation pipelines, vector databases, and AI-powered document processing. The goal is to build scalable workflows that solve practical problems in knowledge retrieval, document analysis, and AI-assisted automation.

02

Key responsibilities

  • Build and experiment with open-source LLM and SLM pipelines.
  • Design and implement retrieval-augmented generation systems.
  • Develop pipelines for documents, PDFs, and structured data.
  • Build ingestion, chunking, embedding, and indexing workflows for documents and datasets.
  • Work with vector databases to support semantic search and retrieval.
  • Implement prompt engineering, model evaluation, and response optimization.
  • Evaluate models, embedding approaches, retrieval methods, and strategies to reduce hallucinations.
  • Explore emerging techniques in vision-language models.
  • Work with engineers to integrate AI components into applications and document technical findings.
  • Participate in weekly discussions on AI architecture decisions and improvements.

03

Qualifications

Required qualifications

  • Strong understanding of machine learning, neural networks, and deep learning fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with large and small language models.
  • Strong proficiency in Python.
  • Experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or Hugging Face Transformers.
  • Experience with LangChain or a similar orchestration framework.
  • Understanding of vector databases, embeddings, similarity search, indexing, and metadata filtering.
  • Experience with document parsing, PDF extraction, and data preprocessing.
  • One to three hands-on AI projects, such as a RAG assistant, document AI system, or LLM application.
  • GitHub profile with links to AI/ML projects.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience with vision-language models or multimodal AI systems.
  • Familiarity with distributed or GPU inference optimization.
  • Open-source contributions, AI hackathons, or ML/NLP research experience.
  • Experience building production-style AI APIs or deploying models with Docker or cloud platforms.
  • Familiarity with LLM inference servers such as vLLM, TGI, or Ollama.
