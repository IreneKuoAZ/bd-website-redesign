Federal Contracts

GSA MAS

A governmentwide schedule for commercial IT products, software, cloud solutions, and professional services at pre-negotiated terms.

Explore GSA MAS

GSA 2GIT

A GSA vehicle for IT hardware, software, and related ancillary services, designed to simplify agency technology buys.

Explore GSA 2GIT

SEWP V

NASA’s governmentwide vehicle for computers, networking, storage, software, and other IT products and product-based services.

Explore SEWP V

ADMC-3

Army CHESS’s contract for enterprise IT hardware, software, maintenance, and services supporting Army and Department of Defense missions.

Explore ADMC-3

ITES-4H

An Army enterprise contract for commercial IT hardware, software, and incidental services, available to eligible federal buyers.

Explore ITES-4H

SEAPORT

The Navy’s SeaPort contract vehicle for engineering, technical, and program-management support across its mission areas.

Explore SEAPORT

State Contracts

CMAS

California’s Multiple Award Schedule gives eligible public entities access to competitively bid IT products, software, and services.

NYSOGS

New York State’s Office of General Services contracts provide state and local buyers a streamlined route to approved technology solutions.

WSCA-NASPO

A cooperative purchasing program that lets state and local agencies use competitively sourced technology contracts across participating states.
