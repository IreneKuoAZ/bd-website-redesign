Better Direct President Mark Evans met with Senator Sinema at Veterans Round Table

October 11, 2023

Better Direct AI President Mark Evans joined Senator Kyrsten Sinema for a veterans round table focused on the experience of veteran-owned businesses and the role they play in public-sector service.

As a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business and HUBZone-certified company, Better Direct AI brings a mission-oriented perspective to technology sourcing, procurement, and implementation. The conversation reflected the importance of opening practical paths for veteran entrepreneurs, local teams, and small businesses serving government customers.

The discussion also aligned with Better Direct AI's broader work: helping agencies and organizations access the right technology through established contracts, trusted manufacturer relationships, and hands-on delivery support.

Veteran-owned businesses bring operational discipline, service experience, and community commitment to public-sector technology delivery.

Better Direct AI continues to invest in contract access, technology partnerships, and local opportunity while supporting customers across federal, state, and commercial environments.
