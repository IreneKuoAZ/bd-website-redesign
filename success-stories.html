Empowering Mission-Critical Connectivity: Better Direct Elevates Wireless Infrastructure at Joint Base Charleston

Better Direct recently partnered with the 628th Communications Squadron (628 CS) at Joint Base Charleston to deliver a seamless, state-of-the-art wireless upgrade that enhances mission readiness and operational efficiency. By modernizing the base's wireless infrastructure, Better Direct not only strengthened connectivity across critical areas but also earned an outstanding CPARS rating, reflecting exceptional performance and reliability..

  • project location

    Joint Base Charleston

  • Mission partner

    628th Communications Squadron

  • deployed

    430 access points

    Advanced wireless infrastructure

Project Highlights

A resilient wireless foundation for everyday operations

From integration through redundancy, every part of the upgrade was designed to maintain continuity across critical facilities.

System Integration

Better Direct integrated the new equipment with the existing second-generation Air Force ARUBA network and BITI program environment.

Equipment Upgrade

Outdated 2G wireless LAN equipment was replaced with 430 advanced access points that meet JITC, FIPS, and TAA requirements.

Improved Performance

Enhanced bandwidth and stronger signal strength improved wireless performance, including in critical flightline areas.

High Availability & Coverage

Multi-tier network access extended reliable connectivity to flightlines, hangars, offices, warehouses, and other essential areas.

Redundancy & Reliability

Overlapping access-point coverage and primary and backup controllers help minimize interruptions and support operational continuity.

Federal Compliance

The deployment met rigorous security and interoperability requirements while fitting the Air Force's established network environment.
